FAQs

Not just another dental clinic

Straight answers about All-on-6 treatment, prices, travel to Istanbul and aftercare, from the team that plans your smile.

How much does All-on-6 cost in Istanbul?

Your price depends on whether you treat one or both jaws and on the bridge material you choose. As a guide, All-on-6 at Vors typically costs 50–70% less than in the UK, US or Western Europe.

Send us your X-ray for a fixed, all-inclusive quote, with treatment, 5-star hotel and VIP transfers included.

What's included in the all-inclusive package?

Your online consultation and treatment plan, 3D scans and X-rays, implant surgery with sedation, temporary and final teeth, your 5-star hotel stay, VIP transfers between airport, hotel and clinic, a personal coordinator and a written guarantee with aftercare.

Is the price fixed, or can it change when I arrive?

Your quote is fixed once your surgeon has reviewed your X-ray or CT scan. If the 3D scan in Istanbul shows something the X-ray couldn't, we explain it and agree any change with you before treatment. Nothing is added without your approval.

How do I pay, and is a deposit required?

A small deposit secures your treatment dates and hotel. The balance is paid at the clinic, by card, bank transfer or cash, and you receive a full itemised invoice.

Why is treatment in Türkiye so much more affordable?

Lower clinic running costs, laboratory fees and overheads, not lower standards. We use the same premium implant brands and digital technology as top Western European clinics, with our own in-house lab.

Is the online consultation really free?

Yes. Send your X-ray or a few photos of your teeth and our implant surgeons prepare a personalised plan and fixed quote within 24 hours, with no obligation to book.

Can I get a quote without an X-ray?

Clear photos of your smile give us enough for an estimate. An OPG (panoramic) X-ray or CT scan lets your surgeon confirm bone levels and give you a fixed price.

What is All-on-6, and how is it different from All-on-4?

Both replace a full arch of teeth with a fixed bridge. All-on-6 uses six implants instead of four, so the bite force is shared across more supports. That means extra stability, a stronger foundation for a full set of teeth and more security if one implant ever needs attention.

Am I a good candidate for All-on-6?

All-on-6 suits people who have lost most of their teeth, have many failing teeth or wear loose dentures. Your surgeon confirms suitability from your X-ray or CT scan and your medical history.

I've been told I don't have enough bone. Can I still have implants?

Often, yes. Angled implant placement, bone grafting or a sinus lift can create the support you need. Your surgeon reviews your scan and explains the safest option for you.

Is the treatment painful?

Implant surgery is performed under local anaesthesia with sedation, so most patients feel little more than pressure. Mild swelling for a few days is normal, and we give you everything you need to recover comfortably in your hotel.

How soon will I have fixed teeth?

In most cases you receive fixed temporary teeth within a few days of surgery, before you fly home. Your final zirconia teeth are fitted on your second trip, once the implants have healed.

Which implant brands do you use?

We use premium implant systems from internationally recognised manufacturers. You receive an implant passport with the brand, model and position of every implant, so any dentist can look after them in future.

How long do All-on-6 implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, dental implants can last for decades. The bridge on top may need maintenance over the years, just like natural teeth.

Zirconia or acrylic: which final bridge should I choose?

Monolithic zirconia is the strongest, most natural-looking and most stain-resistant option, and the one we recommend for most All-on-6 patients. Acrylic bridges cost less but wear faster. Your surgeon will help you choose.

Can I have both jaws treated on the same trip?

Yes. Many patients choose All-on-6 on both jaws in one trip, so they heal once and return once for their final teeth. It usually adds only a day or two to your stay.

How long do I need to stay in Istanbul?

Most All-on-6 patients make two trips. The first takes 5–7 days for implant surgery and fixed temporary teeth. After 3–6 months of healing at home, the second trip of 7–10 days is for your final custom-made zirconia teeth.

I live in Romania. How easy is it to get to Vors?

Very easy. There are daily direct flights from Bucharest to Istanbul, taking about an hour and a half. Your driver meets you at arrivals and a Romanian-speaking coordinator looks after you throughout your stay.

Which airport should I fly into?

Either Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen (SAW). Send us your flight details and your private driver will be waiting at arrivals with your name.

Can a family member or friend come with me?

Of course. Your companion can share your hotel room and all VIP transfers. Just tell your coordinator when you book.

Where will I stay?

In a hand-picked 5-star hotel a short drive from the clinic, with breakfast included. Your coordinator arranges check-in and every transfer between hotel and clinic.

Do I need a visa to travel to Türkiye?

Many visitors, including citizens of Romania and most EU countries, can enter Türkiye visa-free for short stays; others can apply online for an e-Visa. Rules change, so please check the official requirements for your passport before you book flights.

Will someone speak my language?

Your coordinator stays with you from your first message to your final check-up. We look after patients in English and Romanian, and can arrange interpreters for other languages.

Are the implants guaranteed?

Yes. Every treatment comes with a written guarantee and aftercare plan, and your coordinator stays in touch long after you fly home.

What happens if I have a problem after I fly home?

Message your coordinator on WhatsApp with photos. Our surgeons review them, advise you remotely and, if needed, arrange a visit to Istanbul under the terms of your guarantee.

What can I eat after surgery?

Soft foods for the first weeks while your implants heal: soups, eggs, fish, pasta and yoghurt. Once your final zirconia teeth are fitted, you can enjoy almost anything again.

How do I look after my new teeth?

Brush twice a day, clean under the bridge with a water flosser or interdental brushes, and have a professional clean every six months. We show you exactly how before you leave.

Can my local dentist do my check-ups?

Yes. Your implant passport and treatment notes give your local dentist everything they need, and our team is happy to speak with them directly.

Will anyone be able to tell they're not my real teeth?

Very unlikely. Your teeth are digitally designed to suit your face, and zirconia is shaded to look like natural enamel. Most patients say friends simply notice they look happier.

Still have a question?

Message our patient coordinators on WhatsApp, in English or Romanian. Most replies arrive within minutes.

Treatments

Which treatment suits your eyes?

Most of our patients have keratopigmentation. Your ophthalmologist tells you which option fits your eyes, including when the answer is none of them.

Best for brown or dark eyes, an exact shade, a single trip

A new colour, layered over your iris

Our main treatment. A femtosecond laser makes a fine ring-shaped tunnel in the cornea, and a micronised mineral pigment in your shade is placed inside it. The centre stays clear for vision and the iris is never touched.

“I expected a long day. It was twenty minutes, then lunch by the water.”

Tom H., keratopigmentation, Ireland
See what a trip includes

Best for lighter, natural tones, no added pigment, patience

Gradual lightening, without pigment

A low-energy laser reduces some of the brown pigment on the front of the iris, so lighter tones underneath show through over the following weeks. It's a newer technique with limited long-term data: the final shade can't be chosen exactly, it can't be reversed, and eye-pressure checks are part of every plan.

“They were upfront that the result is gradual, not a set shade. That honesty sold me.”

Daniel S., laser lightening, Netherlands

Keratopigmentation is meant to last. A shade can be deepened or adjusted, and pigment can be partly removed, but complete reversal can't be guaranteed, so treat it as a permanent decision. Laser lightening can't be reversed.
